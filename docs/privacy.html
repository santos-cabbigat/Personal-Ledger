Personal Ledger

Privacy policy

Google account and workbook data

Personal Ledger uses your Google account email to identify you when you open a workbook and to record who posted an entry. It reads the accounts, journal entries, and settings in your selected Google Sheet to display balances and reports. It writes the entries and changes you submit.

Google permissions

The app requests Google Sheets access so you can choose an existing ledger and update its records. Drive access lets the app find or create the ledger files it manages. The spreadsheet interface permission is used for the ledger menu, sidebar, and dialogs inside Google Sheets.

Storage and access

Ledger records are stored in your Google Sheet. Google Apps Script stores the workbook identifiers and posting preferences used to reconnect your account. People with access to the workbook can see records according to its Google Drive sharing settings, including the poster information recorded with entries.

The website and exports

GitHub Pages serves the app interface and the sample ledger. The live app sends workbook requests to Google. Its browser client keeps its Google access token in the open page’s memory. When you download a report, a copy is saved to the destination you choose.

Your controls

You can remove the app’s access in your Google Account connections. Ledger entries remain in the workbook until someone with the appropriate access changes or deletes them. To correct or remove an entry in a shared workbook, contact the workbook owner. The owner can also change who has access in Google Drive.
