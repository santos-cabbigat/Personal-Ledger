Personal Ledger

Privacy policy

Your sign-in

Supabase Auth handles your email address, password and account confirmation. Your browser stores a renewable session so you can reopen the ledger. Use Settings → Sign out on a shared device. Login passwords are never copied to Google Sheets.

Journal and reports

The owner’s personal Supabase project stores the live books, accounts, entries and posting history. The app checks your confirmed email and ledger membership before returning records. GitHub Pages serves the interface and sample data.

Daily Google Sheet archive

The workbook owner’s scheduled Google Apps Script copies journal revisions and account catalogs to the private workbook. It reads the written rows back before confirming archival. The archive includes dates, amounts, descriptions, references and poster identities. Access to the workbook follows its Google Drive sharing settings.

Cleanup and downloads

The owner can replace verified journal detail in the database with dated accounting summaries. The original Sheet archive stays available. Downloads are saved to the destination you choose.
